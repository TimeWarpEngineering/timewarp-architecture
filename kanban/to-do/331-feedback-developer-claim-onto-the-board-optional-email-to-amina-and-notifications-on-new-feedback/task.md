# Feedback: developer claim onto the board, optional email to Amina, and notifications on new feedback

## Description

Today the only action on a feedback item in the admin panel is changing its status. Steven wants more ways to act on feedback:

1. **Developer claim:** a way for Steven to mark himself as the developer, claim feedback items, and put them on the kanban board.
2. **Optional email to Amina:** optionally email feedback to Amina (Steven's Grok Bot, timewarp@mail.grokbot.com). Amina has Akili (Intelligence) research it, then Busara (Wisdom) or Clef decides the next move.
3. **Notifications:** more generally, create a notification when feedback arrives so someone can subscribe to it and take action, rather than status change being the only option.
